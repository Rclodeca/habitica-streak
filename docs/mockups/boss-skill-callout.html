Boss skill callout

Scrolled down to the habits, where you usually are when a hit sets off a skill. Pick a skill, then replay.

A · Card

A modal over everything; tap OK to close. You can't miss it, and it stops you mid-list. With "first time" on, it gets a New tag.

B · Banner

Drops in under the pinned bar as the hit lands, holds 3s (thin timer line), then slides away. Tap to close early. Doesn't block taps below it.

C · Bar takeover

No new element: the bar's name line becomes the skill for 3s, with a shake and a red glow. Quietest one, but also the easiest to miss.

D · Lasting markers

An add-on to any of the above. A fired diamond turns into its skill's icon and stays. The boss modal lists what fired; markers still waiting stay hidden.

Suggested mix: A the first time you ever meet each skill (with the New tag), B every time after that, and D always.

Failed (Hushing Bell) uses the same callout, greyed out: "Rend · Failed". Doom landing at rollover would join the rollover popups rather than use the banner.
